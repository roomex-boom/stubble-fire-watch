The Problem Context

Why Predict Post-Monsoon Agricultural Burning?

Following the Kharif rice harvest between late September and late November, farmers in Punjab and Haryana have a narrow window of 10 to 20 days to clear fields before sowing Rabi wheat. Open-field stubble burning releases massive quantities of PM2.5, carbon monoxide, and greenhouse gases into the Indo-Gangetic Plains, triggering severe air quality emergencies across Northern India.

Early, accurate district-level fire predictions provide state pollution control boards and district administrators with actionable intelligence to deploy mechanical seeders, fire tenders, and enforcement teams before widespread burning occurs.

ML Prediction Pipeline
1. Multi-Source Integration
CREAMS ground bulletins + FIRMS satellite anomalies + Paddy rice area + MODIS burned area.
2. Temporal & Lag Feature Engineering
1-day, 2-day, 3-day satellite and ground fire lags + 3-day/7-day rolling statistics without future leakage.
3. Chronological Model Training
Trained across 5 algorithms (Dummy Baseline, Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting).
4. Real-Time District Advisory Inference
Predicts count, classifies risk level (Low, Moderate, High, Severe), and outputs administrative advisories.
